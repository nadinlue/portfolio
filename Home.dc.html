About

I’m a Berlin-based artist working across visual development, concept art, and games marketing.

I enjoy shaping a project’s visual identity, exploring what makes it distinctive, and bringing characters, environments, and ideas into a coherent whole.

My approach combines hands-on art with an eye for the bigger picture. I work closely with teams across disciplines, balancing creative ambition with the practical needs of production—from the first explorations to the final artwork.
